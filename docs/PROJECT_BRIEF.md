# PROJECT_BRIEF.md

## Vision
A fast-loading, Hindi-first, culturally respectful, richly animated mobile wedding invitation that feels alive without feeling chaotic. The invocation establishes the sacred opening, Rachna and Udit are the emotional focus, the two confirmed ceremonies are impossible to miss, and every future detail slots into a prepared content model.

## Audience
- Family, relatives and friends receiving the link via WhatsApp/SMS/social apps, overwhelmingly on phones (mid-range Android and iPhone), often on variable networks.
- Mixed age groups; many prefer Hindi; some prefer English; some use larger system fonts.

## Narrative arc
1. **Sacred opening** — `॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥`, Ganesha line art draws in.
2. **Couple reveal** — रचना × उदित enter from opposite sides, joined by a floral connector.
3. **Invitation** — "सप्रेम आमंत्रण" with the family message.
4. **Ceremonial timeline** — a line draws as the guest scrolls.
5. **शुभ तिलक** — 25 Nov 2026.
6. **शुभ विवाह** — 2 Dec 2026.
7. **Countdown** — days only.
8. **Future modules** — venue, directions, calendar, RSVP, family, gallery, music (hidden until data exists).
9. **Share** — native share / copy link.
10. **Closing blessing**.

## Confirmed wedding data
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Couple | Rachna & Udit |
| शुभ तिलक | Wednesday, 2026-11-25 |
| शुभ विवाह | Wednesday, 2026-12-02 |

## Explicitly unknown (do not invent)
Event times, venues, parent/host names, RSVP contacts, dress code, muhurat, accommodation, photographs, music, additional ceremonies.

## Inspiration (market context)
Current digital-invitation products combine traditional stationery cues with animated presentation, shareable links, rich event info and RSVP tooling. This project borrows the *capabilities* (sharing, event details, RSVP-readiness) while using an Indian ceremonial visual language: ornament, paisley/henna forms, mandalas, florals, metallic accents, rich traditional hues.

## Out of scope (for now)
Guest-list management, payments, user accounts, CMS, analytics beyond basic privacy-respecting metrics.
